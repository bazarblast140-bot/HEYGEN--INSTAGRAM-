Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Paise Ki Pathshala Uploader is used by its owner to publish educational finance videos to a YouTube channel.

Google and YouTube data

The application requests only the Google/YouTube permissions needed to upload and manage the owner's authorized YouTube videos. OAuth credentials and tokens are used for authentication and are stored in protected application secrets.

Use of information

Google and YouTube data is used only to provide the requested upload and publishing functionality. The application does not sell, rent, or use Google user data for advertising.

Sharing

Google/YouTube data is not shared with third parties except where required to operate the authorized publishing workflow or by law.

Data retention

OAuth credentials are retained only as long as needed for the publishing automation. The owner can revoke Google access at any time through Google Account security settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer contact email configured in Google Auth Platform.
